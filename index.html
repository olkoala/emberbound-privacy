Emberbound Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Emberbound is a mobile game available for iOS and Android. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use Emberbound.

1. Information Emberbound stores

Emberbound stores game progress and related gameplay data locally on your device.

Emberbound currently does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, or phone number in order to play the game.

Local game data may include information such as:

  • game progress;
  • unlocked features and upgrades;
  • gameplay settings;
  • timestamps used for gameplay and offline progression;
  • other data necessary to save and restore your game.

This gameplay data is stored on your device and is not used by Emberbound for advertising profiling.

2. Advertising

Emberbound uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

Emberbound currently uses rewarded advertisements. Rewarded advertisements are optional advertisements that a player may choose to watch in exchange for an in-game benefit.

When advertisements are requested or displayed, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • device information;
  • IP address;
  • advertising identifiers, where available and permitted;
  • approximate location derived from information such as an IP address;
  • information about advertisement impressions and interactions;
  • diagnostic and technical information;
  • consent and privacy choices.

The exact information processed by Google may depend on your device, region, privacy settings, and consent choices.

Google processes information according to its own privacy policies and advertising policies.

3. Consent and European privacy requirements

For users in regions where consent is required, Emberbound uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy choices before requesting advertisements where required.

Depending on your region, you may be asked whether you consent to certain uses of your personal data for advertising purposes.

You may also be given options to:

  • consent;
  • decline consent;
  • manage individual privacy choices;
  • change your privacy choices later.

Emberbound uses the consent status provided by Google's consent platform to determine whether advertising requests are permitted.

4. Personalized and non-personalized advertising

Depending on your consent choices, location, and applicable law, Google may serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

Personalized advertisements may use information about your interests or activity to select advertisements.

Non-personalized advertisements are generally selected using contextual information rather than a profile of your interests, although certain data may still be processed for purposes such as frequency capping, fraud prevention, measurement, security, and basic ad delivery.

5. Third-party services

Emberbound currently uses the following third-party service for advertising and consent management:

Google AdMob / Google User Messaging Platform
Provider: Google LLC

These services may process information independently according to Google's own terms and privacy policies.

Emberbound does not control how Google or its advertising partners process information once it is handled by their services.

6. Data sharing

Emberbound does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by Google and participating advertising partners when advertisements are requested or displayed.

7. Data retention

Game progress stored by Emberbound remains on your device until it is removed, reset, overwritten, or otherwise deleted through use of the app or your device.

Information processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's own data-retention practices.

8. Your privacy choices

Where required or supported, Emberbound provides access to privacy choices through Google's consent system.

You may be able to review or change your advertising privacy choices from within the app once the relevant privacy-management option is available.

9. Children's privacy

Emberbound does not knowingly request users to provide personal information directly through an Emberbound account system.

Advertising and privacy requirements may differ depending on a user's age and jurisdiction. Emberbound uses the privacy and advertising configuration provided through Google AdMob and applicable platform settings.

10. Security

Emberbound takes reasonable steps to keep its implementation and locally stored game data secure.

However, no software or data-storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Emberbound's features, advertising providers, data practices, or legal requirements change.

The date at the top of this policy will be updated when material changes are made.
